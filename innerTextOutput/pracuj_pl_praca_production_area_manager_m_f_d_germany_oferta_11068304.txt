Recruitment for
Our client is an internationally active manufacturing company in the wood industry with multiple locations worldwide. The company is one of the leading providers in its sector. With state-of-the-art technology, high-quality standards and a clear growth strategy, our client has been setting benchmarks in the market for years. For an important production site in Eastern Germany, we are seeking an experienced and motivated leadership personality to significantly contribute to the optimization of production processes and the development of the team:
Production Area Manager (m/f/d)
Drive production excellence with strong leadership – Your opportunity to make an impact!
Your responsibilities
Responsibility for the production area with a strong focus on quality and output optimization
Lead a production area comprising approximately 7 direct reports and over 50 team members, ensuring seamless operations across multiple production lines
Analyzing and improving processes, minimizing waste and costs, and ensuring adherence to standards and quality guidelines
Contribute to investment planning and long-term strategic decisions, execute management directives and track performance against set objectives
Handling technical issues, optimizing machine settings, and creating and maintaining technical documentation
Responsibility for occupational safety, environmental standards and cleanliness, particularly in a 24/7 continuous production environment
Employer requirements
Degree in process engineering, wood technology, materials science, mechanical engineering or chemistry; alternatively, equivalent qualifications as a technician or master craftsman
Several years of experience in a production environment, ideally in the wood or paper industry
Strong leadership personality with passion for innovation, assertiveness and resilience in challenging situations
Proficient German and English skills
What employer offers
A varied and responsible leadership position in a dynamic company with a leading market position
Competitive, performance-driven salary complemented by extensive training and professional development programs
International onboarding at other company locations
Diverse career opportunities, national and international
Additional benefits such as pension plans, top-up payments, meal subsidies, and attendance bonuses
